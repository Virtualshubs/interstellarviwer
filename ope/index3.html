The challenge

Our world is constantly changing. Understanding it means seeing beyond the data.

Every observation reveals part of a much larger picture. Its real value emerges when we can connect those pieces and understand what they mean together.

From satellite imagery and orbital activity to three-dimensional environments, spatial information is produced across countless sources, formats and scales. Yet these resources often remain isolated, making it difficult to explore their full potential.

SEOD develops technologies that bring these different dimensions together, creating new ways to explore, interpret and work with the world around us.

    OUR STORY

    From digital twins to spatial intelligence.

    SEOD originated from our work in digital twin technologies. While developing and managing large volumes of data to feed our models, we encountered a recurring challenge: the difficulty of accessing, connecting and exploiting complex datasets efficiently.

    This challenge led us to identify an opportunity beyond our original field. The nature of our technology and the advances achieved through our internal development made it possible to apply these capabilities to the space and geospatial data ecosystem.

    SEOD emerged from that transition: bringing together spatial computing, data engineering and proprietary technology to develop new ways of working with Earth observation and georeferenced information.
