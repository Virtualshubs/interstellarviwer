The challenge

Spatial data is growing. Making sense of it requires new ways of computing.

The value of observation is not only in what we collect, but in the relationships we are able to reveal.

Spatial information is distributed across different sources, formats and systems. Understanding its relationships requires more than simply accessing or visualizing individual datasets.

SEOD addresses this challenge through technologies designed to connect spatial information with its computational context.
